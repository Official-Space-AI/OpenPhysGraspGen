# Support

OpenPhysGraspGen has two levels of support: community support, which is free and
open to everyone, and commercial support, which SpaceAI plans to offer under
contract.

| | Community support | Commercial support |
|---|---|---|
| Status | Available now | Planned, not offered yet |
| For | Anyone using the open parts: the package, the dataset, the benchmarks and the container image | Organizations using SpaceAI's commercial offerings, or the open parts in their own products |
| Where | GitHub Discussions and Issues, and the dataset's Community tab on Hugging Face | A private channel set up with each customer |
| Response | First triage within 48 hours on weekdays, then best effort | Response and resolution times agreed in the support contract |
| Covers | Questions, bug reports and fixes in public releases, documentation | Help with integration and deployment, priority fixes, and help with the commercial offerings |
| Cost | Free | Under contract |

## Community support

[COMMUNITY.md](COMMUNITY.md) says where to ask what, and how the maintainers triage
new issues and discussions. Answers are public, so that the next person with the same
question finds them. Community support is not a support contract.

Security vulnerabilities are never reported in public. Follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Commercial support

SpaceAI plans a paid support tier for organizations that rely on the project in their
work, together with the commercial offerings listed in
[docs/governance/open-core.md](docs/governance/open-core.md). It is not offered yet.
When it is, this page will say what it includes and how to start.

Until then, write to **oss.spaceai@gmail.com** if your organization needs support
beyond what the community channels give, or a commercial license for the dataset.
